Duo Party – Support

Face-to-face party games for two on one iPhone. Made for iPhone Duo, playable on any iPhone.

Contact

Email admin@pagewalker.website. We usually reply within two working days.

How do we play on iPhone Duo?

Face-off: open the Duo and stand it up. One player uses the inner screen, the other the outer screen on the back. Table: lay it open and flat between you. The app picks the right mode for each game.

No iPhone Duo?

Every game works on any iPhone with pass-and-play: a curtain hides the screen between turns, and real-time games split the screen.

Restore my Duo Party Pass

Tap any locked game, then “Restore purchases”. The Pass is shared with your Family Sharing group.

Cancel the subscription

Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Duo Party Pass › Cancel. You keep access until the end of the paid period.
