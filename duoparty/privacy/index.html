Duo Party – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Duo Party collects no data. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and makes no network requests of its own.

Names and scores you enter stay on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Camera. In face-off mode on iPhone Duo, the camera shows a live view of the player across from you. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Purchases. The Duo Party Pass subscription is handled by Apple. We receive no payment or personal details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to the App Store.

Children. The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Contact: open an issue or reach the developer via the App Store page.
