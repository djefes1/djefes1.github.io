Duo Party – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Short version: Duo Party has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The games work offline.

Names and scores you enter stay on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Camera. In face-off mode on iPhone Duo, the camera shows a live view of the player across from you. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Purchases. Payments for the Duo Party Pass subscription are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details. To show the subscription offer and check whether your Pass is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID created on your device and your App Store purchase receipts (product, price, dates). This data is used only to provide the subscription, is not linked to your name or contact details, and is not used for advertising or tracking.

Children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. To ask about subscription data, email us.
